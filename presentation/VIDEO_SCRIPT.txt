----------------------------------------------------------------
DELIVERY NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------

THE ONE WORD TO LAND IS "SAME".
   "written by the SAME FUND that holds the equity"
   Everything before it is setup. Everything after is evidence.
   If you land one word in sixty seconds, land that one.

SAY "two-million-dollar", NOT "two million dollars".
   It scans faster and sounds deliberate.

DO NOT SAY "AI" OR "LLM".
   The extraction is table stakes. The finding is the product.
   Naming the model invites accuracy questions you don't need
   in a 60-second video.

THE 14-SECOND INGEST IS YOUR BIGGEST PROBLEM.
   That is a quarter of your runtime watching a spinner.
   Do not narrate through it. Best options in order:
     1. Record continuously, cut the dead time in post.
     2. Speed-ramp the wait to 2-3 seconds with the
        processing state still visible.
     3. Only if you must keep it real-time, fill it with the
        entity-resolution line - but that costs you the
        liquidity beat.
