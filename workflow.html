01 — Inventory

Everything is one of two things. Keeping them straight is most of the architecture.

EXTERNAL

Arrives from outside. Episodic, untrusted, uncontrolled.
Fund capital statementsQuarterly · 45–75 day lag
Capital call noticesIrregular · 10-day fuse
Distribution noticesIrregular
Property appraisalsAnnual · often stalest data
Loan / servicer schedulesMonthly–quarterly · covenants
Custody statementsThe liquid book — the denominator
Market pricesContinuous · moves value with no doc
LLM APIHard dependency · plan for failure

INTERNAL

State you own. Cumulative, curated, compounding.
Entity graphNodes + typed edges — the product
Alias corpusThe moat · grows per document
Commitment registerTotal / drawn / undrawn · notice periods
Policy thresholdsCoverage 3× · concentration · staleness
Confidence calibrationTrust per GP, per field
Analyst overridesCorrections · confirmed merges
External data is episodic and untrusted. Internal state is cumulative and curated. Every arriving document is a claim to be reconciled against what you already know — never a row to be appended.

TRAVERSAL & COMPUTE

Look-through: for each entity, sum over all inbound paths from the family office root — Σ (Π edge weights) × leaf value — grouped by edge_type, so equity, debt and guarantee exposure to one company surface together. This is the whole bet.

06

OUTCOME

Five things, and only five:

1 · The coverage ratio. One number, always current. 0.92× vs 3.00× standard.
2 · Ranked findings by severity, in plain language.
3 · Change alerts — fired when a threshold is crossed, not on every recompute. Alert on the transition or you train people to ignore you.
4 · Provenance — every figure links to source document and date. An unauditable number is worthless in a risk tool.
5 · The review queue — low-confidence extractions and proposed merges awaiting a human.

03 — Three clocks

The same pipeline runs on three rhythms. Design for all three or it feels broken.

01

QUARTERLY

▸ STATEMENTS ARRIVE IN BATCH

45–75 days stale on arrival. Full pipeline, recompute everything.

“Your picture has been updated. Three things changed.”

02

EVENT

▸ CAPITAL CALL NOTICE · 10-DAY FUSE

Fast path: update the commitment, recompute coverage immediately.

“This call takes you to 0.71×. Here's what you'd have to sell.”

03

CONTINUOUS

▸ NOTHING ARRIVES · MARKETS MOVE

Re-shock the liquid book only. The ratio drifts with no document at all.

The clock people miss. A system that only recalculates on document arrival is blind between quarters — exactly when a drawdown happens.

04 — Worked example

The Aurex finding, traced through all seven stages.

01
Two documents arrive from two different counterparties: Kestrel LP report (as-of 31-Mar) and Blackfin loan schedule (as-of 30-Jun). Different senders, different formats, 91 days apart.
02
From Kestrel — equity holding, “Aurex Data Centers, Inc.”, 15% of $3,060,000, plus a prose “Sponsor Support” note about unsecured guarantees. From Blackfin sheet 2 — loan BF-2025-002, $2,000,000, unsecured, “guarantee from lead sponsor (Kestrel Ventures Fund III LP)”.
03
Resolver unifies the two Aurex references into one node. And resolves “Kestrel Ventures Fund III LP” in Blackfin's text to the existing Kestrel fund node — a cross-document, cross-counterparty link.
04
Three edges written: holds_equity (FO→Kestrel→Aurex) · holds_debt (FO→Aurex, $2,000,000, unsecured) · guarantees (Kestrel→Aurex debt).
05
Traversal groups all three by entity: total Aurex exposure $2,459,000. Then detects the guarantor node is itself a node the family office holds equity in — correlated credit support.
06
Finding emitted: “$2,459,000 exposure to Aurex across two asset classes. The guarantee backing the unsecured $2M loan is written by Kestrel Ventures Fund III — the same fund you own the equity through. One credit event impairs the equity, leaves the loan unsecured, and weakens its only credit support.” Provenance: 2 documents, 3 extracted facts.
07
Analyst confirms. Kestrel Ventures Fund III LP ↔ Kestrel Ventures Fund III, L.P. enters the alias corpus permanently.
What this required: two documents, two counterparties, 91 days apart, one fact buried on a second worksheet and one in a prose footnote — connected through a typed edge that isn't a holding at all. No table produces this. No fund-layer look-through produces this.
